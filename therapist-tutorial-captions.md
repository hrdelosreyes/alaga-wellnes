Companion to `therapist-tutorial-video-scripts.md`. One caption per episode per platform.

## Platform rules of thumb

| Platform | Style | Link | Hashtags |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Facebook Reels** | Longer, conversational, 1–2 emojis per line, ends with the CTA | Paste `alagawellness.care` — clickable in caption | 3–5, at the end |
| **Instagram Reels** | Medium; hook in the first line (only ~125 chars show before "more") | Links aren't clickable → "link sa bio" | 5–8, at the end |
| **TikTok** | Short, punchy, hook-led; searchable keywords | "link sa bio" | 3–5, mix of broad + niche |

## Always include (compliance)

- Any post that mentions earnings, referral or bonus: add **"Hindi garantisadong kita."**
- Say **weekly payout**, never "instant" or "agad".
- Referral / bonus = **"up to" / "kung qualified"**.
- Don't promise "pwede na agad magtrabaho" — approval takes 3–5 business days and a city must be live.
- Pinned comment on every post: *"Mag-apply dito: alagawellness.care 💆‍♀️ Tanong? I-comment lang!"*
