## BRAND QUICK-REFERENCE

> Apply these consistently across all designs for a cohesive campaign.

### Colors
| Role | Color Name | Hex |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Primary (deep) | Forest Green | `#1B4D3E` |
| Accent (warm) | Gold | `#C9A84C` |
| Background (light) | Warm Cream | `#F7F3ED` |
| Text on dark | Off-White | `#FAF8F4` |
| Text on light | Charcoal | `#2C2C2C` |
| Overlay tint | Deep Green 80% opacity | `#1B4D3E` at 80% |

> 🎨 **Note:** If you have official brand hex codes from your designer, use those. The above are matched to the warm, premium Philippine wellness aesthetic of the site.

### Typography
| Use | Font | Style |
|-----|------|-------|
| Headlines | **Playfair Display** or **Cormorant Garamond** | Bold |
| Body / Subheads | **Inter** or **DM Sans** | Regular / Medium |
| Taglish accent lines | **DM Sans** | Italic or Bold |

> All fonts are free in Canva.

### Logo
- Use `logo.png` (light version) on dark/green backgrounds
- Use `logo-dark.png` on cream/white backgrounds
- Always place in top-left or centered at bottom — never crowded

### Photo Style
- **Real people** — Filipino therapists, natural expressions, not stock-photo stiff
- **Warm lighting** — soft, golden hour indoor tones
- **Settings:** Home interiors, condo living rooms, massage table setups
- Avoid: Clinical/hospital looks, overly bright studio lighting

## GENERAL CANVA PRODUCTION TIPS

1. **Create a Brand Kit in Canva** — add the hex codes above so every design auto-applies them
2. **Use Canva's "Resize" feature** — design the 1080×1080 FB/IG first, then resize to 1080×1920 for TikTok/Reels and adjust
3. **Save each design as a template** — so future city-specific variants are one edit away
4. **Photo sourcing:** Use your own therapist photos for authenticity. If using stock, search "Filipino massage therapist home" on Canva or Pexels. Filter for Southeast Asian models.
5. **Video for TikTok/Reels:** Film yourself or a therapist speaking directly to camera, then add the text overlays using TikTok's built-in editor (not Canva) for native feel
6. **Export settings:**
   - Facebook / Instagram: PNG, max quality
   - TikTok video frames: MP4 (if animated in Canva) or PNG for static
