## POSTING STRATEGY

### Recommended Frequency
| Platform | Frequency | Best Times |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| Facebook | 3x/week | Tue–Thu, 7–9PM |
| Instagram | 4x/week (2 feed, 2 Reels) | Mon/Wed/Fri, 6–8PM |
| TikTok | 5x/week | Daily, 7–10PM |

### Suggested Rotation (Week 1)
- **Mon:** TikTok Script 1 + IG Post 1
- **Tue:** FB Post 1 (post in therapist groups)
- **Wed:** TikTok Script 2 + IG Reel Caption
- **Thu:** FB Post 2 + IG Post 2
- **Fri:** TikTok Script 3
- **Sat:** FB Post 3 + IG Post 3
- **Sun:** TikTok Script 4 (income math — high shares on weekends)

### Group Targeting (Facebook)
Post FB Post 1 and 4 directly in these types of groups:
- "TESDA graduates Philippines"
- "Massage therapists Philippines"
- "Home service / freelance wellness PH"
- City-specific: "Trabaho sa [City]", "[City] wellness professionals"

### City-Specific Strategy
Use **FB Post 4** as a template — fill in the city name and post city by city as you expand. This creates urgency ("slots filling up in your area") and feels personal.
