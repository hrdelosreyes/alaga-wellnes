## FAQ CONTENT

---

### 🟢 GETTING STARTED

**Sino ang pwedeng mag-apply sa Alaga Wellness?**  
Anyone who meets the following:
- May valid NBI Clearance (or willing to secure one before onboarding)
- TESDA-certified in massage therapy (any specialization: Swedish, Shiatsu, Hilot, Deep Tissue, etc.)
- Professional, punctual, and client-focused
- Available to do home service within their city

We welcome therapists from **all cities across the Philippines** — not just Metro Manila.

---

**Libre ba mag-apply?**  
Yes, completely free. There are no application fees, no membership fees, and no upfront costs to join the platform. You only start earning when you start completing bookings.

---

**Gaano katagal ang application process?**  
Typically 3–5 business days from submission. Here's how it works:

1. **Submit your application** — fill out the form with your details, certifications, and availability
2. **Document verification** — we review your NBI Clearance and TESDA certificate
3. **Profile setup** — once approved, we help you set up your therapist profile and rates
4. **First booking** — you're live and ready to receive clients

We'll email you at each step so you're never left wondering where things stand.

---

**Kailangan ko bang pumunta sa isang opisina?**  
No. The entire application and onboarding process is done online. Once approved, you work directly from your home city — no commute to a head office required.

---

### 💰 EARNINGS & PAYMENT

**Magkano ang kikitain ko per session?**  
You set your own rates within Alaga's service range:
- Alaga Relax (60 min): from ₱505
- Alaga Hilot (75 min): from ₱560
- Alaga Recovery (90 min): from ₱725

The more sessions you complete, the more you earn. Many of our active therapists complete 3–5 sessions per day.

---

**Ilang porsyento ang napupunta sa akin?**  
You keep the majority of what the client pays. Alaga takes a small platform share to cover operations, booking technology, payment processing, and client support. The exact breakdown is shared with you during onboarding when you set your rates — before you accept your first booking.

---

**Paano ako mababayaran?**  
Payments are cashless and processed through the platform. Your earnings are released after each confirmed and completed session. Payout details (GCash, bank transfer, etc.) are set up during your profile onboarding.

---

**May referral earnings ba?**  
Yes. If you refer another therapist to Alaga and they start completing bookings, you earn a referral bonus every time they complete a session — even when you're resting. It's a way to grow your income beyond your own sessions.

---

**Ano ang Quarterly Alaga Bonus?**  
Every quarter, Alaga distributes a bonus pool among therapists who have been consistently active — showing up, completing bookings, and maintaining high client satisfaction. If you're an active, reliable therapist, you'll receive a share of this bonus on top of your regular session earnings. Full details are shared during onboarding.

---

### 📅 SCHEDULE & FLEXIBILITY

**Pwede ba akong pumili ng sarili kong schedule?**  
Yes — completely. You set the days and hours you're available. You can be available 7 days a week or just weekends. You can take breaks, mark yourself unavailable, and adjust your schedule anytime through your therapist portal.

---

**May minimum number ba ng sessions na kailangan ko per week?**  
There is no strict minimum for staying on the platform. However, to qualify for the Quarterly Alaga Bonus and maintain your active status, we encourage consistent availability. Therapists who show up regularly earn more and receive priority placement in client searches.

---

**Pwede ba akong magwork sa maraming lungsod?**  
Yes, as long as you're able to travel safely to the client's location. During onboarding, you'll set your service area — typically a radius around your home city. You can update this anytime.

---

**Sino ang nagtatakda kung sino ang client ko?**  
Clients choose you based on your profile, rating, and availability — so your reputation builds your bookings. You can also see client details before accepting a booking. If for any reason you need to decline, you can do so through the platform.

---

### 🛡️ SAFETY & VERIFICATION

**Paano ko malalaman na ligtas ang client?**  
Every client on Alaga creates a verified account with a registered contact number and payment method. You can view their booking details — location, session type, and client profile — before accepting. You'll also receive real-time support from the Alaga team during all active bookings.

---

**Bakit kailangan ng NBI Clearance?**  
NBI Clearance is required for the safety of both therapists and clients. It's also a key part of what makes Alaga a trusted platform — clients book with confidence knowing every therapist has been vetted. If your NBI is expired, we can guide you on how to renew it online.

---

**Makikita ng clients ang aking personal na impormasyon?**  
Clients see your first name, profile photo, specializations, rating, and general service area. Your personal address, phone number, and full legal name are never shared publicly.

---

### 📱 USING THE PLATFORM

**Paano ko matatanggap ang bookings?**  
Once your profile is live, you'll receive booking notifications via the Alaga therapist app or portal. You'll see the client's location, session type, and scheduled time. You can accept or decline each booking.

---

**Kailangan ko bang magdala ng sariling kagamitan?**  
Yes — therapists bring their own professional kit (massage oils, linens, portable table if applicable). Alaga specifies what clients should expect you to bring as part of the service standard, and this is outlined during your onboarding.

---

**Paano kung may problema during a session?**  
The Alaga support team is available 8AM–10PM daily via hello@alagawellness.care. For urgent concerns during an active session, contact us immediately. Your safety is always our priority.

---

**Maaari ba akong mag-offer ng ibang services bukod sa listed?**  
Currently, bookings are structured around our core services (Alaga Relax, Alaga Hilot, Alaga Recovery). As we expand — including Postpartum Care and Senior Care — approved therapists with relevant specializations will be invited to offer these services first.
