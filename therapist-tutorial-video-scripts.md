# Alaga Therapist Tutorial Series — Scripts & Shot Lists

Series: **"Paano Kumita sa Alaga"** · 6 episodes · FB Reels / IG Reels / TikTok

## Global production notes

- **Format:** 9:16 (1080×1920), captions burned in (Taglish), safe zone — keep text out of the top 150px and bottom 350px (platform UI).
- **Voice:** warm, simple Taglish. One speaker (a real therapist on camera for the hook/outro is ideal) + voiceover over screen recordings.
- **Screen recordings:** record the real portal on a **demo therapist account** with fake name/numbers. Never show a real therapist's GCash/bank number, NBI, or address. Use phone-sized viewport so text is readable.
- **Brand:** Alaga logo bug top-left (use Canva logo page 1), end card with `alagawellness.care`.
- **Pacing:** hook in first 2 seconds, cut every 2–4 seconds, zoom/highlight the button or field being discussed.
- **Music:** soft upbeat instrumental, ducked under voice. Use platform-licensed audio only.
- **Pinned comment / caption CTA on every episode:** "Mag-apply sa alagawellness.care — link sa bio."
- **Release timing:** hold public posting until at least one city is live, or use "founding therapist" wording. No city is live as of Oct 2026.

### Claims guardrails (must appear in every relevant episode)
- Take-home is **75%** of each completed session. Alaga's 25% covers operations, booking tech, payments, support.
- Payouts are **weekly, sent manually** to the saved GCash/Maya/bank account (suggested ₱500 minimum). Never say "instant".
- Referral commission (5% per level, 2 levels, up to the referred therapist's first 100 bookings) and the Quarterly Alaga Bonus (15+ completed bookings in the quarter) are **"up to" / "kung qualified"** — not typical earnings. Add on-screen disclaimer: *"Hindi garantisadong kita. Depende sa bookings."*
- Example numbers (current prices): Relax ₱505 → **₱379** to you · Hilot ₱560 → **₱420** · Recovery ₱725 → **₱544**.
- Rates shown are guide prices; each city has its own range and you set your rate within it.
